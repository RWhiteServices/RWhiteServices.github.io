Our Origin Story

Every summer, Colorado’s mountain towns come alive with hikers, road-trippers, and families looking to experience everything the area has to offer. Along the way, we noticed that grabbing a great cup of coffee often meant settling for either convenience or quality. Locals wanted a café they could count on, and visitors deserved a place that felt welcoming instead of rushed. That’s what inspired Mountain Bean Café. We wanted to create a space where people could slow down before heading out on the trail, enjoy coffee that’s been thoughtfully sourced and roasted, and feel like they were genuinely welcomed—not just another order in line.

From the beginning, our goal has been simple: serve exceptional small-batch coffee with friendly, consistent service every single day. We carefully source our beans, roast them to bring out balanced, approachable flavors, and train our team to make every guest feel at home. Whether you’re visiting for one morning during a mountain getaway or keeping Mountain Bean in your routine through our coffee subscription, you can expect the same thing every time—a fresh cup of coffee, crafted with care, and an experience that keeps you coming back.
