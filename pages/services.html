Three W's Bar — Services

Three W's Bar is a boutique event service from Rachael White Services offering mobile bar experiences, curated boutique clothing, and fun event add-ons for weddings, parties, corporate even[...]

What we offer

  • Mobile Bar Services — Professional bartenders, full mobile bar setup, signature cocktails, mocktails, and beverage packages tailored to your event size and style.
  • Boutique Clothing — Curated selection of dresses, accessories, and themed attire for hosts and guests; styling and rental options available.
  • Event Extras — Bar-themed decor, cocktail menu design, glassware rental, bar staff, and coordination with caterers and venues.
  • Custom Packages — Wedding packages, party packages, and corporate/event bundles that combine bar services, attire, and add-ons at a discounted rate.

How booking works

Tell us about your event (date, guest count, venue, and style). We'll recommend a package or build a custom offering to fit your needs and budget. We handle setup, service, and teardown so you can enjoy the event.
