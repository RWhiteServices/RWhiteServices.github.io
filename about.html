A website serves many different purposes and does not simply exist to share information. It should serve the audience and grab their attention, build a community, and encourage customers to engage. Websites also serve a strong purpose in working hard for their communities around them. However, the website may not always portray who they seek to help, what their business can really do, what they need to solve, and lastly, what the next step would be for customers. Having confusing layouts, headings, and not having a clear call to action can affect the website's success in many ways. This also leads to many customers leaving the site or becoming disengaged before they can take their next step. The website needs to be conducted in a way that addresses the concerns of the customer right away, and shows that their business understands the needs of the customer. This is one of the most important aspects of a website and is what allows a business to grow and learn from the experiences that the customers are having.
